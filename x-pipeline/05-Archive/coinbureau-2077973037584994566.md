**Status: True.** 
Trump Media & Technology Group (TMTG) announced on July 16, 2026, the launch of a paid, licensed data service called **"Truth API"** (also referred to in some reports as "Truth PSI"). The service is scheduled to launch on August 1, 2026, and is designed to provide financial institutions, banks, and algorithmic trading firms with faster-than-standard, real-time access to posts from the platform's most influential accounts, which includes President Donald Trump.

### 2. Key Facts & Numbers
*   **Service Name:** Truth API.
*   **Launch Date:** August 1, 2026.
*   **Function:** Provides a licensed data feed of posts from Truth Social’s 10 most influential accounts (including President Trump) significantly faster than standard public push notifications.
*   **Target Audience:** Algorithmic trading firms, hedge funds, banks, and financial institutions.
*   **President Trump's Reach:** 12.9 million followers on Truth Social.
*   **Trump's Stake:** President Trump owns approximately 41% of Trump Media through a revocable trust.

### 3. Comparative Baseline Data
*   **Current Status Quo:** Historically, financial firms have relied on manual monitoring or standard web scraping to track market-moving posts on Truth Social, creating latency that can be exploited by faster automated systems.
*   **Market Context:** Other platforms (e.g., X, Reddit) offer API access for data, but the unique controversy here stems from the platform's primary user being the President of the United States, whose posts frequently impact national security, trade policy, and global financial markets.

### 4. Macro/Geopolitical Context
Truth Social has become a primary channel for U.S. presidential communications, including announcements on tariffs, trade restrictions, and geopolitical tensions (e.g., the Iran war). By monetizing access to these communications, TMTG is effectively creating a premium tier for "information asymmetry," where institutional investors with the capital to pay for the API can front-run the general public on information that moves markets.

### 5. Most Affected Party & Impact
*   **Primary Beneficiaries:** Trump Media & Technology Group (new revenue stream) and institutional traders/hedge funds (who gain a millisecond-level competitive advantage).
*   **Most Affected:** Retail investors and the general public, who receive information later and may be forced to trade against algorithms that have already reacted to the information. 
*   **Financial Flow:** Fees paid by institutional subscribers flow directly to TMTG, thereby increasing the value of the company in which President Trump holds a 41% stake.

### 6. Secondary/Hidden Effects
*   **Regulatory/Ethics Scrutiny:** Ethics experts have labeled this a potential conflict of interest, as the President is essentially profiting from the market volatility generated by his own official communications.
*   **Market Volatility:** The existence of such a feed may incentivize even more aggressive algorithmic reactions to presidential posts, potentially increasing short-term market instability.
