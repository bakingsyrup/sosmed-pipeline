The company is also heavily exposed to geopolitical risks, including Starlink's role in global conflict zones and the reliance on U.S. government contracts. The "AI trade" is currently unwinding, and SpaceX—a massive consumer of AI compute and capital—is viewed as a proxy for the sustainability of this broader tech spending cycle.

### 7. Secondary/Hidden Effects
