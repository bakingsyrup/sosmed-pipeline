#### 5. Secondary/Hidden Effects
*   **The "Term Premium" Trap**: The market is increasingly demanding a higher premium to hold long-term US debt due to fiscal concerns. This creates a feedback loop: higher yields increase the US government's interest expense, which increases the deficit, which requires more borrowing, which puts *more* upward pressure on yields.
*   **Crowding Out**: As the government competes for capital at 5%+ yields, it becomes harder and more expensive for private companies to borrow, potentially slowing private sector innovation and capital expenditure.
