# Fact-Checking & Research Brief
### 1. Verification Status
**True.** SK Hynix released its Q2 2026 earnings on July 29, 2026, reporting record-high results that nonetheless fell short of market expectations, triggering a significant sell-off in both its stock and the broader KOSPI index. The phrase "Pray for Kospi" was used in financial media (notably ZeroHedge) to capture the market's reaction to the volatility.

### 2. Key Facts & Numbers
*   **Operating Profit**: 60.5 trillion KRW (~$41.6 billion), representing a 557% increase year-over-year.
*   **Earnings Miss**: Analysts had forecasted an operating profit of ~64 trillion KRW.
*   **Revenue**: 79.3 trillion KRW, missing the consensus forecast of ~84 trillion KRW.
*   **Net Profit**: 93.9 trillion KRW, a 1,242% increase year-over-year (boosted by non-operating gains).
*   **Market Impact**: The KOSPI index fell 10.8% on July 28, 2026, closing at 6,023.66. SK Hynix stock experienced extreme volatility, including a record intraday plunge.

### 3. Comparative Baseline Data
*   **Previous Year (Q2 2025)**: SK Hynix reported an operating profit of 9.2 trillion KRW.
*   **Previous Quarter (Q1 2026)**: Operating profit was 37.6 trillion KRW.
*   **Forecasts**: Analysts (e.g., LSEG SmartEstimate, FnGuide) had priced in higher growth, anticipating revenue of 83.9 trillion KRW and profit near 64 trillion KRW.

### 4. Historical Precedents & Past Benchmarks
*   **Category A (Historical Event Anchor)**: The July 2026 KOSPI sell-off (dropping ~10.8% in one session) is among the most severe single-day declines in recent memory, reminiscent of liquidity-driven panics during past semiconductor cycle downturns.
*   **Category B (Parallel Industry Playbook)**: The "AI Bubble" skepticism parallels the 2000 Dot-com bust or the 2018 crypto winter, where massive capital expenditure (CapEx) promises by hyperscalers (Microsoft, Meta, etc.) are increasingly scrutinized for their ability to generate near-term ROI.
*   **Category C (Entity Track-Record Anchor)**: SK Hynix recently completed a record $26.5 billion US ADR listing (July 10, 2026) to tap into US capital, but the subsequent earnings miss caused the stock to trade below its IPO price, testing investor confidence in its growth narrative.

### 5. Macro/Geopolitical Context
The sell-off reflects a "reality check" on the AI infrastructure cycle. Hyperscalers have committed massive capital (e.g., ~$750 billion in AI-related funding), but investors are questioning whether this spending is sustainable. Additionally, rising competition from Chinese entities like CXMT (which recently debuted in Shanghai) is pressuring the dominant position of Korean chipmakers.

### 6. Most Affected Party & Impact
*   **Losers**: South Korean retail and institutional investors (KOSPI heavyweights like SK Hynix and Samsung Electronics account for over 50% of the index weighting).
*   **Financial Flow**: Massive capital rotation out of semiconductor "AI-winners" into safer assets or cash as the "AI supercycle" narrative faces its first major stress test.

### 7. Secondary/Hidden Effects
*   **Margin Compression**: SK Hynix's focus on high-end HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) meant it benefited less from the sharp price surge in conventional DRAM/NAND compared to competitors.
*   **Systemic Trigger**: The sell-off triggered "sidecar" trading curbs (circuit breakers) in South Korea, highlighting the fragility of a market heavily concentrated in a single sector.
