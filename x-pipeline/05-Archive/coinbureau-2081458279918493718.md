# Fact-Checking & Research Brief
### 1. Verification Status
**True.** BNY (formerly BNY Mellon) has formally announced plans to enable 24/7 settlement for both conventional and tokenized U.S. Treasuries by 2027. The bank will initiate private blockchain pilots for tokenized Treasuries by the end of 2026. This follows successful after-hours test transactions involving stablecoin issuers (Ripple's RLUSD and OpenEden's USDO) conducted earlier in 2026.

### 2. Key Facts & Numbers
*   **Assets Under Custody/Administration (AUC/A):** $62.6 trillion (as of Q2 2026).
*   **Assets Under Management (AUM):** $2.2 trillion (as of Q2 2026).
*   **Target Timeline:** 
    *   **2026 (Year-End):** Launch of private blockchain pilot for tokenized Treasuries.
    *   **2027:** Targeted rollout of 24/7 settlement for conventional and tokenized Treasuries.
*   **Current Infrastructure Impact:** BNY is the dominant triparty agent for U.S. repo, supporting $24.3 trillion in average daily clearance and processing $2.5 trillion in payments each day.

### 3. Comparative Baseline Data
*   **Historical Context:** Traditional U.S. Treasury settlement has historically been constrained by the operating hours of the Federal Reserve’s Fedwire Securities Service.
*   **Market Pressure:** The growth of stablecoins and tokenized Treasury funds—which operate 24/7—has created a "weekend lag" where reserve assets cannot be adjusted or settled outside of traditional banking hours.

### 4. Historical Precedents & Past Benchmarks
*   **Category A (Historical Event Anchor):** The transition to **T+1 settlement** in the U.S. (May 2024) served as the most recent major infrastructure overhaul in securities settlement, aimed at reducing counterparty risk and margin requirements.
*   **Category B (Parallel Industry Playbook):** BNY’s move mirrors the broader adoption of "always-on" payment rails (e.g., The Clearing House’s RTP network or FedNow), which have already begun moving the banking industry toward real-time, 24/7 settlement for cash.
*   **Category C (Entity Track-Record Anchor):** BNY has consistently positioned itself as the "balance sheet of choice" for digital assets, acting as the primary reserve custodian for major stablecoins (USDC, RLUSD, USDO) and testing tokenized deposit balances (launched January 2026).

### 5. Macro/Geopolitical Context
This initiative represents a defensive and strategic response by the traditional financial system to "crypto-native" infrastructure. By integrating 24/7 settlement, BNY is ensuring that the U.S. Treasury market—the bedrock of global liquidity—remains the primary collateral for the burgeoning digital asset and stablecoin ecosystem, preventing a "decoupling" of digital asset liquidity from traditional sovereign debt markets.

### 6. Most Affected Party & Impact
*   **Gainers:** Stablecoin issuers and digital asset firms (gain capital efficiency and reduced settlement risk); BNY (reinforces its status as the "hub" of the financial system).
*   **Losers:** Traditional intermediaries reliant on slow, legacy clearing windows; potential reduction in the utility of "off-chain" or non-Treasury-backed stablecoin alternatives.
*   **Financial Flow:** Capital currently sitting idle in stablecoin reserves during weekends will become more mobile, potentially increasing the velocity of money within the digital-asset-to-Treasury pipeline.

### 7. Secondary/Hidden Effects
*   **Liquidity Concentration:** As BNY settles on its own books for "inside the tent" counterparties, it potentially creates a closed-loop liquidity pool that operates independently of the central bank during off-hours, shifting systemic risk from the Fedwire network to BNY’s internal balance sheet.
*   **Programmable Collateral:** Once Treasuries are tokenized, they can be used as "atomic" collateral in smart-contract-based lending protocols, potentially sparking a surge in institutional DeFi activity.
