# Fact-Checking & Research Brief
### 1. Verification Status
**True.** Phantom wallet has officially announced it will discontinue support for the Monad network. While some reports cite August 12 and others August 26, the consensus in official and industry-reported updates confirms the deprecation is imminent in August 2026. Users are being advised to migrate their assets to other EVM-compatible wallets.

### 2. Key Facts & Numbers
*   **Deadline:** August 12 – August 26, 2026 (Discrepancy in reporting suggests August 12 as the safer, functional deadline).
*   **Integration Duration:** Approximately 9 months (Launched November 2025; sunsetting August 2026).
*   **Action Required:** Users must import their secret recovery phrases into alternative EVM-compatible wallets (e.g., MetaMask, Rabby) or swap Monad-native assets for supported tokens.
*   **Asset Safety:** Assets remain on-chain and secure; the change is limited to the Phantom interface's ability to display or interact with the Monad network.

### 3. Comparative Baseline Data
*   **Historical Context:** Phantom integrated Monad in November 2025, coinciding with the Monad mainnet launch. The rapid reversal (9 months) is atypical for major wallet integrations but reflects the competitive "multi-chain" wallet landscape where support is often prioritized based on transaction volume and user activity.

### 4. Historical Precedents & Past Benchmarks
*   **Category A (Historical Event Anchor):** Similar to past wallet "pruning" events (e.g., when major wallets have deprecated support for less-active chains or testnets to focus on high-traffic networks like Solana, Ethereum, or Base).
*   **Category B (Parallel Industry Playbook):** "Aggressive Acquisition, Selective Pruning." Wallets frequently support new, hyped L1s to capture initial user traffic (Airdrop/Mainnet hype) but prune them if they fail to sustain "sticky" transaction volume or if the maintenance cost of the integration outweighs the revenue from swaps/fees.
*   **Category C (Entity Track-Record Anchor):** Phantom has historically maintained a "Solana-first" focus while expanding to EVM chains (Ethereum, Polygon) and Bitcoin. This move signals a strategic shift back toward concentrating resources on high-liquidity ecosystems.

### 5. Macro/Geopolitical Context
This move reflects the ongoing "L1 Consolidation" phase in the crypto market. As the number of L1 blockchains proliferates, infrastructure providers (wallets) are increasingly acting as gatekeepers, forcing users toward a smaller set of high-liquidity, high-security ecosystems.

### 6. Most Affected Party & Impact
*   **Most Affected:** Retail users who rely solely on the Phantom interface for their Monad holdings.
*   **Financial Flow:** Potential for "Liquidity Migration" away from the Monad ecosystem if users find the migration process too cumbersome, leading to sell-offs of MON tokens in favor of more "stable" assets on supported chains.
*   **Winners:** Competing EVM wallets (MetaMask, Rabby) that gain user-base share as Phantom users migrate.

### 7. Secondary/Hidden Effects
*   **UX Friction:** The "interface migration" may lead to temporary spikes in support requests and phishing attempts, as users are prompted to import recovery phrases into new applications.
*   **Ecosystem Signaling:** The exit of a major, user-friendly wallet like Phantom can be perceived as a negative signal regarding the health or long-term growth of the Monad ecosystem by institutional or retail observers.
