# Fact-Checking & Research Brief
### 1. Verification Status
**True.** Subversive ETFs filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) on July 8, 2026, to launch two actively managed ETFs: the **Nasdaq-100 Ex-Elon Enterprises ETF (QQNE)** and the **S&P 500 Ex-Elon Enterprises ETF (SPNE)**. These funds are designed to track their respective indices while systematically excluding companies "founded, controlled, or led by" Elon Musk, specifically Tesla (TSLA) and SpaceX (SPCX). The funds are expected to begin trading on or around September 21, 2026.

### 2. Key Facts & Numbers
*   **The Trigger:** SpaceX's rapid inclusion in the Nasdaq-100 following its June 2026 IPO. Nasdaq’s rules allow for fast-tracked inclusion of mega-cap companies, forcing passive index funds to purchase the stock.
*   **Fund Mechanics:** Both ETFs are actively managed and will maintain at least 80% of their assets in the respective index exposures (Nasdaq-100 or S&P 500), minus the "Excluded Enterprises." The weightings of the removed companies (Tesla/SpaceX) will be redistributed across the remaining index constituents based on market capitalization.
*   **Contextual Performance:** Since its inclusion in the Nasdaq-100, SpaceX stock has reportedly declined by approximately 17% (as of mid-July 2026).

### 3. Comparative Baseline Data
*   **Benchmark ETFs:** Standard index trackers like the Invesco QQQ Trust (QQQ) generally have very low expense ratios (e.g., ~0.18%). Because the new Subversive ETFs are "actively managed" to filter out specific stocks, they are expected to have higher expense ratios, which may erode long-term returns compared to traditional passive index funds.
*   **Historical Precedent:** Industry analysts (e.g., from ETF.com) have compared these filings to previous "gimmick" or thematic funds, such as the now-defunct "Inverse Jim Cramer" ETF or Subversive's own former Metaverse ETF (PUNK), which struggled to attract significant assets and were eventually closed.

### 4. Macro/Geopolitical Context
The move reflects an increasing trend of "personalized" or "ideological" investing. Investors who are wary of Musk’s polarizing public profile, political activities (e.g., support for specific candidates, labor practices, or management style), and the volatility associated with his firms are seeking ways to decouple their broad market exposure from his influence. This highlights a growing friction between passive index investing (which is "blind" to corporate governance/personality) and values-based retail demand.

### 5. Most Affected Party & Impact
*   **Retail Investors:** They are the primary target audience. Those who want exposure to the Nasdaq/S&P 500 but object to Musk-linked volatility or politics gain a tool to express that preference. However, they risk higher fees and potential tracking error compared to standard indices.
*   **Subversive ETFs:** The firm gains potential fee income and brand visibility, though it faces the risk of low liquidity and fund closure if the products fail to gain institutional or significant retail scale.
*   **Index Constituents:** The exclusion does not "hurt" Tesla or SpaceX directly in terms of capital flow, as these ETFs are likely to be small relative to the massive AUM of traditional index funds.

### 6. Secondary/Hidden Effects
*   **Tracking Error:** By removing major components like Tesla (and potentially others in the future), these funds will inevitably deviate from the performance of the actual Nasdaq-100 or S&P 500, which could lead to investor dissatisfaction if the excluded stocks outperform the rest of the market.
*   **Market Fragmentation:** The proliferation of such "niche" index funds contributes to the fragmentation of the ETF market, potentially confusing retail investors who may not fully understand the risks of active management versus passive tracking.
*   **Governance Signaling:** While these funds may not move the needle on stock prices, they signal a growing market appetite for "ESG-like" filters that are based on specific leadership profiles rather than traditional environmental or social metrics.
