# Fact-Checking & Research Brief
### 1. Verification Status
**Status: True.**
Google launched a new generative AI feature in Google Earth on July 30, 2026, which allowed users to overlay AI-generated imagery onto real-world satellite, aerial, and 3D maps via text prompts. The feature was withdrawn by the company on July 31, 2026—less than 24 hours after launch—following widespread reports of users creating and circulating "deepfake" satellite imagery depicting disasters, military sites, and other fabricated scenarios.

### 2. Key Facts & Numbers
*   **Launch Date:** July 30, 2026.
*   **Rollback Date:** July 31, 2026 (within 24 hours).
*   **Technology:** The feature was powered by Google’s **Nano Banana 2** image-generation model.
*   **Mechanism:** Users could zoom into any location on the web version of Google Earth, tap "create image," and enter a prompt to generate photorealistic overlays.
*   **Policy Context:** Google stated that while the generated images were watermarked (using SynthID) and not visible to others in the main Google Earth interface, the capability allowed for the rapid creation of realistic-looking misinformation that could be screenshotted and shared externally.
