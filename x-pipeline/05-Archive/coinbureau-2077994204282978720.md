# Fact-Checking & Research Brief
### 1. Verification Status
**True.** MegaETH has officially confirmed the sunsetting of its "Mega Mafia" accelerator program after two years and two cohorts. The decision was announced by core team member Shuyao Kong on July 16, 2026.

### 2. Key Facts & Numbers
*   **Program Duration:** Two years, covering two cohorts.
*   **Startups Supported:** Approximately 20 teams.
*   **Funding Raised:** Incubated teams collectively raised ~$80 million in funding (from pre-seed through Series A).
*   **Equity/Governance:** MegaETH took **zero** equity or governance rights in the incubated projects.
*   **Strategic Pivot:** MegaETH is shifting resources toward "first-party applications" and "OMEGA" applications (consumer-facing apps designed specifically for its infrastructure).

### 3. Comparative Baseline Data
*   **Previous Model:** The program relied on an "alignment-based" model where MegaETH provided technical, management, and market-making support without formal ownership, assuming projects would remain loyal to the network.
*   **Outcome:** Several high-profile projects abandoned the ecosystem:
    *   **GTE (Global Token Exchange):** Building its own chain.
    *   **Noise:** Migrated to Base.
    *   **HelloTrade:** Moved to Monad.
    *   **Others:** Projects like Avon and Valhalla ceased operations.

### 4. Macro/Geopolitical Context
This event highlights a maturing trend in the blockchain sector where "ecosystem bootstrapping" strategies—often involving heavy subsidies and non-equity incubation—are being re-evaluated. As liquidity tightens, protocol foundations are moving away from "growth at all costs" (via external builders) toward "value capture" models (first-party development) to ensure the protocol’s long-term economic sustainability.

### 5. Most Affected Party & Impact
*   **MegaETH (The Protocol/Foundation):** The primary entity losing out. It spent millions of dollars on market-making, audits, and engineering support for projects that ultimately exited the ecosystem, resulting in a net-negative return on investment (ROI) regarding ecosystem stickiness.
*   **Independent Developers/Founders:** The primary beneficiaries. They received significant technical and financial support (bootstrapping) from MegaETH without surrendering equity or governance control, allowing them the flexibility to migrate to other chains once their projects matured.

### 6. Secondary/Hidden Effects
*   **"Ecosystem Churn":** The ease with which successful projects left suggests that without equity or technical lock-in, "founder goodwill" is insufficient to prevent migration to competing chains that may offer better incentives or technical fit.
*   **Shift to Vertical Integration:** By moving to first-party apps, MegaETH is attempting to mirror the "Apple-style" vertical integration—controlling both the hardware/infrastructure and the applications—to ensure value remains within the network.
