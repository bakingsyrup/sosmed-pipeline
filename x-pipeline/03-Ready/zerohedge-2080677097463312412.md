By taking a massive salary sacrifice, James is prioritizing "basketball happiness" and competitive utility over total career earnings. This strategy effectively subsidizes the team's ability to retain or acquire other high-salary stars, shifting the balance of power in the Eastern Conference.

*   **Market Distortion**: By signing for the veteran minimum, James creates an artificial constraint on the market for other veteran players, as his presence on a "super-team" at a discount price raises the bar for what constitutes a "contender," potentially forcing other teams to over-leverage their cap space to compete.
