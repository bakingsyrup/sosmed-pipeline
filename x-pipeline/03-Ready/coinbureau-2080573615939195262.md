# Fact-Checking & Research Brief
### 1. Verification Status
**Status: True.**
The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) officially announced that it will host a public roundtable on **September 17, 2026**, at its headquarters in Washington, D.C. (File Number 4-913), to discuss the transition to 24-hour trading in U.S. equity markets. The event will be livestreamed on SEC.gov.

### 2. Key Facts & Numbers
*   **Event Date:** September 17, 2026.
*   **Context:** Major exchanges are actively seeking to extend trading hours to compete with the 24/7 nature of crypto markets.
*   **Key Players/Exchanges Involved:**
    *   **Nasdaq:** Aiming for 23-hour trading, five days a week, with a target launch of December 6, 2026.
    *   **NYSE Arca:** Planning 23-hour weekday trading for 2026.
    *   **Cboe:** Planning near-24/5 trading, pending regulatory approval.
    *   **Blue Ocean ATS:** Currently provides overnight U.S. stock trading (operational since 2021).
    *   **London Stock Exchange (LSE):** Developing a 24/5 trading venue ("LSE 24"), with trials expected by late 2026 and a launch in the first half of 2027.

### 3. Comparative Baseline Data
*   **Current Standard:** Traditional U.S. equity market hours are generally 9:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. ET. While pre-market and after-hours trading exist, the "national equity market" does not currently operate as a continuous, unified 24/7 system.
*   **Market Pressure:** The shift is driven by the demand for "always-on" finance, mirroring the infrastructure of cryptocurrency markets, which have operated 24/7 since inception.

### 4. Macro/Geopolitical Context
This move represents a fundamental modernization of U.S. market infrastructure. It aims to align U.S. equity markets with global, continuous trading environments. The transition is not merely about "longer hours" but requires a total overhaul of clearing, settlement, surveillance, margin systems, and broker-dealer risk controls to ensure operational resilience in a market that never closes.

### 5. Most Affected Party & Impact
*   **Retail Investors:** Gain the ability to react to news events outside of traditional hours, potentially reducing "gap risk" (where prices jump between market close and open).
*   **Broker-Dealers/Market Makers:** Face significant operational pressure. They must maintain liquidity and provide customer support, surveillance, and risk management 24/7, which increases overhead costs.
*   **Clearing Houses:** Must adapt systems to handle continuous settlement and collateral requirements.

### 6. Secondary/Hidden Effects
*   **Liquidity Fragmentation:** If trading occurs across extended hours on different venues, liquidity could become fragmented, potentially increasing volatility or widening bid-ask spreads during low-volume overnight sessions.
*   **Algorithmic/Agentic Trading Dominance:** 24/7 markets are highly conducive to automated, AI-driven, and "agentic" trading strategies, potentially marginalizing human traders who cannot monitor markets around the clock.
