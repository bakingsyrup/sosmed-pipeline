# Fact-Checking & Research Brief
### 1. Verification Status
**Status: True.**
The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) officially announced that it will host a public roundtable on **September 17, 2026**, to discuss the transition of U.S. equity markets toward 24-hour trading. The roundtable (File Number 4-913) will focus on the operational, technical, and regulatory requirements for supporting overnight trading and ensuring market resilience.

### 2. Key Facts & Numbers
*   **Event Date:** September 17, 2026.
*   **Focus Areas:** Infrastructure, clearing/settlement, broker-dealer overnight controls, operational resilience, and investor protection.
*   **Market Developments:** Several major exchanges are actively pursuing extended hours:
    *   **Nasdaq:** Targeted to launch 23-hour trading (five days a week) on December 6, 2026.
    *   **NYSE Arca:** Approved for 22-hour trading; preparing for 23-hour weekday operations.
    *   **Cboe:** Seeking approval for near-24/5 trading, aiming for late 2026.
    *   **London Stock Exchange (LSE):** Planning a night-time trading venue for early 2027.
    *   **Blue Ocean ATS:** Has been operating overnight U.S. stock trading since 2021.

### 3. Comparative Baseline Data
*   **Traditional Hours:** Since 1985, major U.S. exchanges have operated on a fixed schedule of 9:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. ET, Monday through Friday.
*   **Crypto Benchmark:** The cryptocurrency market has functioned 24/7 since its inception, providing a "continuous trading" model that traditional finance (TradFi) is now attempting to emulate to remain competitive.

### 4. Macro/Geopolitical Context
This shift represents a move toward "always-on" global financial infrastructure. As global capital flows become increasingly electronic and borderless, the traditional exchange model is viewed as outdated. By adopting 24-hour trading, U.S. markets aim to capture volume that currently migrates to off-exchange venues or crypto markets during overnight hours, thereby maintaining U.S. market dominance and liquidity.

### 5. Most Affected Party & Impact
*   **Gainers:** Retail investors (who gain flexibility to react to global news outside standard hours) and exchanges (which gain potential trading volume and fee revenue).
*   **Losers/Risks:** Smaller broker-dealers and market makers may face significant costs in upgrading infrastructure (clearing, settlement, surveillance, and staffing) to support overnight operations.
*   **Financial Flow:** Liquidity that currently "leaks" into alternative trading systems (ATS) or crypto-assets during off-hours will likely be consolidated back into major regulated exchanges.

### 6. Secondary/Hidden Effects
*   **Liquidity Compression:** The "crypto premium" for 24/7 access may compress as TradFi offers a regulated alternative, potentially impacting crypto-native exchange volumes.
*   **Systemic Fragility:** A move to 24/7 trading requires the DTCC and NSCC to adapt clearing/settlement cycles; failure to synchronize these systems could lead to increased settlement risk or "flash" volatility during low-liquidity overnight sessions.
