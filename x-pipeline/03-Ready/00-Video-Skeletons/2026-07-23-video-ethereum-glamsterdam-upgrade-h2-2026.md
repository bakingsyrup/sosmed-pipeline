- Verification Details: The "Glamsterdam" upgrade is a confirmed Ethereum hard fork, originally planned for H1 2026 and now rescheduled to **H2 2026 (Q3, with an internal mainnet target of end of August 2026)**. It is the first full base‑layer throughput upgrade since The Merge, focusing on L1 scaling via ePBS, block‑level access lists, and a 200M gas limit. # 📌 1. Topic Idea & Overview
- Subject: Ethereum’s “Glamsterdam” Upgrade – H2 2026
- Summary: After a delay from H1 2026, the Glamsterdam hard fork is set for a late‑August 2026 mainnet activation. The upgrade marks Ethereum’s most aggressive L1 scaling push since The Merge, introducing enshrined proposer‑builder separation (ePBS, EIP‑7732), block‑level access lists for parallel execution (EIP‑7928), and a roadmap to a 200M gas limit. Projected outcomes include a 10,000 TPS theoretical ceiling and a 71‑78% reduction in gas fees, fundamentally repositioning Ethereum from purely an L2 settlement chain toward a balanced, high‑throughput L1 competitor.
