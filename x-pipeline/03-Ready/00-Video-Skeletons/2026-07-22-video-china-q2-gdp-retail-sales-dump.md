- Subject: China’s Q2 2026 GDP Dump & the Domestic‑Export Fracture  
- Summary:  
  China’s Q2 GDP growth slowed sharply to 4.3% (missed 4.5% forecast) while H1 held at 4.7%, still within the official 4.5–5% range. This skeleton unpacks the “K‑shaped” recovery and what it means for traders, consumers, businesses, and policymakers.

## Angle 3 Data & 2nd-Degree Deep-Dive:
- 1st‑Degree Metric: **Youth unemployment 14.9%** in June 2026, with a peak expected above 20% after 12.7 million graduates enter labor market — Source: [Caixin Global](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2026-07-15/china-youth-unemployment-rate-falls-to-149-but-record-graduates-await-102382.html)

## Angle 4 Data & 2nd-Degree Deep-Dive:

- 🔥 2nd‑Degree High‑Signal Fact: **PPI inflation reached 4.1% in June, heavily driven by non‑ferrous metals (+25%) and AI‑chip related components.** This is not broad‑based demand‑pull inflation but a supply‑chain cost push that actually **improves nominal revenue and debt‑servicing capacity** for large SOEs — Source: [NBS PPI release](https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202607/t20260710_1849901.html)

## Angle 5 Data & 2nd-Degree Deep-Dive:
- 1st‑Degree Metric: **Real disposable income +4.2% vs. real consumption +2.7%** in H1 2026 — Source: [NBS Income](https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202607/t20260715_1850127.html)
- 🔥 2nd‑Degree High‑Signal Fact: **Household savings rate jumped to 35% of disposable income (highest since 2022), with bank deposits surging RMB 12 trillion in H1.** The marginal propensity to consume has fallen to just 0.46, meaning for every extra yuan earned, less than half is spent — Source: [PBOC Financial Statistics](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/en/3688247/3688978/3709171/index.html)
