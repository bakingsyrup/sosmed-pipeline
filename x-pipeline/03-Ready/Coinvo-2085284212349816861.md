# Fact-Checking & Research Brief
### 1. Verification Status
**True.** As of August 6, 2026, Coinbase has officially launched 24/5 US stock trading for eligible UK users. This service allows users to trade approximately 4,000 US equities directly through the Coinbase app, with funding options in GBP or USDC.

### 2. Key Facts & Numbers
*   **Asset Coverage:** Nearly 4,000 US stocks.
*   **Trading Hours:** 24 hours a day, 5 days a week (24/5).
*   **Commission:** Zero-commission trading.
*   **Minimum Investment:** Fractional shares starting from £1.
*   **Funding:** Instant funding via GBP or USDC (stablecoin).
*   **Regulatory Basis:** CB Payments Ltd is authorized and regulated by the UK Financial Conduct Authority (FCA).
*   **Strategy:** Part of the "Everything Exchange" initiative, aiming to unify crypto, traditional equities, savings, and lending in a single platform.

### 3. Comparative Baseline Data
*   **US Market:** Coinbase launched this same 24/5 US equity service in the United States in February 2026.
*   **Competitive Landscape:** Coinbase is now directly competing with UK-based and international platforms such as eToro, Freetrade, Hargreaves Lansdown, and Trading 212.
*   **Previous Status:** Prior to this, Coinbase users in the UK were largely restricted to crypto-asset services; the new authorization (received in July 2026) was required to offer traditional securities.

### 4. Historical Precedents & Past Benchmarks
*   **Category A (Historical Event Anchor):** The launch of 24/5 equity trading in the US (Feb 2026) served as the proof-of-concept. Within months, it was expanded to the UK, signaling a rapid geographic scaling strategy for their brokerage model.
*   **Category B (Parallel Industry Playbook):** The "Everything Exchange" model mimics the evolution of firms like Robinhood or eToro, which transitioned from single-asset or limited-asset platforms to multi-asset financial "super-apps" to increase user retention and LTV (Lifetime Value).
*   **Category C (Entity Track-Record Anchor):** Coinbase has consistently prioritized regulatory compliance in the UK, obtaining FCA authorization to provide investment services. This contrasts with earlier, more aggressive expansion attempts in other jurisdictions that faced immediate regulatory friction.

### 5. Macro/Geopolitical Context
The move represents a strategic effort to bridge "on-chain" finance (stablecoins like USDC) with traditional equity markets. By allowing USDC to be used for traditional stock funding, Coinbase is effectively increasing the velocity and utility of its stablecoin ecosystem, potentially reducing reliance on traditional banking rails for retail investors.

### 6. Most Affected Party & Impact
*   **Gains:** 
    *   **Coinbase:** Increases user stickiness and transaction volumes.
    *   **UK Retail Investors:** Gain extended-hour access to US stocks, which was previously limited or unavailable through traditional local brokers.
*   **Loses/Affected:** Traditional UK brokerage firms (e.g., Hargreaves Lansdown) may face pressure to lower fees or extend trading hours to remain competitive.

### 7. Secondary/Hidden Effects
*   **Liquidity Fragmentation:** By moving trading to internal or proprietary electronic systems during "after-hours" sessions, there is a risk of wider spreads and lower liquidity compared to standard exchange hours.
*   **Stablecoin Integration:** The use of USDC as a funding vehicle for stocks creates a "closed-loop" financial ecosystem, potentially increasing stablecoin demand as a collateral and settlement asset.
