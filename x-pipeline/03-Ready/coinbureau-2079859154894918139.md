# Fact-Checking & Research Brief
### 1. Verification Status
**True.** 
MVMT Labs, Inc., the original developer of the Movement blockchain, filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy (Subchapter V) in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware on July 15, 2026. The claims regarding low revenue and the token's price collapse are consistent with financial data and recent reporting. 

### 2. Key Facts & Numbers
*   **Bankruptcy Filing:** MVMT Labs filed for Chapter 11 (Subchapter V) on July 15, 2026.
*   **Financials:** The company listed assets between **$100,001 and $1 million** and liabilities of **$1 million to $10 million**.
*   **Revenue/Fees:** Daily app revenue has remained below **$800** since November 2025, and daily chain fees have recently dropped to as low as **$1**.
*   **Token Performance:** The MOVE token has plummeted over **94%–99%** from its all-time high (ATH), trading at approximately **$0.01**.
*   **Funding:** The project had raised approximately **$41.4 million** in equity financing (including a $38M Series A) and reportedly pursued a Series B round that once targeted a **$3 billion** valuation.

### 3. Comparative Baseline Data
*   **Fundraising vs. Reality:** The project raised over $41 million (with some reports citing up to $141.4M in total capital), yet it struggled to generate sustainable organic revenue, relying on incentives that failed to foster long-term network activity.
*   **Market Context:** The collapse was exacerbated by a market-making scandal in early 2025 where 66 million MOVE tokens were sold, creating approximately **$38 million** in downward price pressure.

### 4. Macro/Geopolitical Context
Movement Labs' failure highlights the "burn-rate" vulnerability of early-stage Layer 2 projects that rely on heavy venture capital funding rather than organic transaction fees. The bankruptcy also underscores the risks of aggressive market-making agreements, which, when opaque, can lead to catastrophic liquidity events and governance crises.

### 5. Most Affected Party & Impact
*   **Retail Investors:** Suffered the most significant losses due to the massive devaluation of the MOVE token.
*   **Creditors:** Includes former co-founder Rushi Manche (largest unsecured creditor with a >$1.6M claim), service providers (auditors like Ottersec), and various operational vendors.
*   **Financial Flow:** Capital flowed from VCs and retail buyers into the project, which was then depleted by high operational costs and market-making activities, leaving the entity insolvent.

### 6. Secondary/Hidden Effects
*   **Entity Separation:** "Move Industries," a separate company formed by former employees in December 2025 to manage the ecosystem, claims to be unaffected by the bankruptcy of MVMT Labs. This creates a "shell" scenario where the original developer is liquidated, but the blockchain continues under new management.
*   **Reputational Contagion:** The scandal has cast doubt on the broader "Move" language ecosystem on Ethereum, leading to increased scrutiny of projects that use similar programming languages but lack sustainable business models.
