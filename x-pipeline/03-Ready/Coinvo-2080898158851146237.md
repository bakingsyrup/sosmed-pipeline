By integrating SK’s manufacturing (HBM) with Nvidia’s compute architecture and sovereign AI objectives, the partnership creates a "vertically integrated AI fortress" that reduces reliance on fragmented global supply chains and hedges against geopolitical volatility in chip production.

#### 6. Most Affected Party & Impact

    *   **SK Group (Hynix/Telecom):** Establishes itself as the indispensable memory partner for the "Golden Age of AI."

*   **Losers:** Competitors in the memory space (if locked out of Nvidia’s roadmap) and other hyperscalers who may face higher costs if Nvidia prioritizes SK-integrated systems.

#### 7. Secondary/Hidden Effects
