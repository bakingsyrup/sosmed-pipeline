# X Glossary Post (English)

MegaETH Just Shut Down Its Flagship Accelerator
The incubated projects raised $80 million, but the protocol took zero equity in return.
In this post, we will touch the basics of Ecosystem Bootstrapping and Value Capture to understand how they form a loop that stalls protocol growth.

Ecosystem Bootstrapping is the process of incentivizing developers and users to join a new blockchain network through grants, subsidies, or accelerator support to reach critical mass. When a new network launches, it is essentially a ghost town. The protocol has to spend heavy capital to attract builders to its ecosystem. It gives out free money, pays for security audits, and provides market-making liquidity so startups can launch their products easily. Think of it like opening a brand new mall. The mall owner gives free rent to big anchor brands for the first year just so regular shoppers start coming in. We saw this when MegaETH funded 20 startups to kickstart its network activity.

However, giving away free money without strings attached leaves the protocol bleeding cash, forcing it to find a way to implement Value Capture.

Value Capture is the ability of a protocol or business model to retain a portion of the economic value generated by its users or applications. Without it, a protocol operates like a charity. It pays for initial growth but gets absolutely nothing back when the projects succeed. The protocol must enforce a mechanism, like transaction fees, token burns, or equity ownership, to sustain its own treasury. Going back to our mall analogy, once the mall is packed with shoppers, the owner must start charging rent and parking fees to make an actual profit. Because MegaETH failed to capture value from its incubated startups, the founders simply left for competing chains, forcing the protocol to shift toward building its own first-party apps to keep revenue in-house.

When a network bootstraps its ecosystem without securing a way to capture value, it simply subsidizes the growth of its competitors. The protocol burns its treasury, and we watch the founders walk away with the liquidity.
