SBI Holdings Acquires Coinhako to Build a Global Digital Asset Corridor

The $230B financial group gains a licensed Singapore exchange with 400k users and an MPI license.

In this post, we will touch the basics of RWA Tokenization and On-Chain Settlement to understand how they form the building blocks of SBI’s cross-border strategy.

Real-World Asset Tokenization is simply taking a physical or traditional financial asset — like a bond or a real estate — and turning it into digital tokens that live on a blockchain. Instead of one person owning the whole bond, the ownership gets split into many tokens, and anyone with a wallet can buy a fraction. It opens up markets that used to be locked away from smaller players, and it lets those tokens move around instantly without waiting for bank paperwork. For example, SBI partnered with Ondo Finance to tokenize traditional assets so they could be traded and settled using JPYSC stablecoins, cutting settlement from days to seconds. This tokenization is useless without a cheap, instant way to move the value, which is where On-Chain Settlement comes in.

On-Chain Settlement is the final step where the value actually moves on the blockchain. Instead of relying on a chain of correspondent banks and a SWIFT message that takes two days, the transaction is cleared and settled directly on the ledger. It’s like sending a text instead of mailing a letter; the confirmation is immediate, and there’s no stack of intermediaries taking a fee. SBI is using this to let companies in Japan and Singapore trade tokenized assets and stablecoins directly through Coinhako. The result: cross-border trade settlements that used to cost time and a cut to multiple banks now happen in seconds, at a fraction of the cost.

Together, RWA Tokenization creates the digital goods, and On-Chain Settlement delivers them. SBI isn’t just buying an exchange — they are laying down a parallel financial highway that bypasses the old toll roads. And the banks still charging those tolls might soon notice the traffic on their roads has disappeared.
