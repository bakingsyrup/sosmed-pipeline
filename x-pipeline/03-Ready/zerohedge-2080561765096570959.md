### 7. Secondary/Hidden Effects
The rise in Japanese yields acts as a "global margin call." Because Japan has historically been the world's largest creditor, rising JGB yields tighten global financial conditions. As Japanese investors sell foreign assets to reinvest in higher-yielding domestic bonds, it exerts upward pressure on yields in the US and Europe, potentially triggering a broader "crowding out" effect in global equity markets.
