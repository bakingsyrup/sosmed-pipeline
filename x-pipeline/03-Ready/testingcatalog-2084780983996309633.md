### 1. Verification Status
**True.** The partnership between SpaceX and NVIDIA was officially announced on August 4, 2026, during SpaceX’s inaugural quarterly earnings call following its IPO. SpaceX confirmed it will use NVIDIA’s Vera Rubin NVL72 platform as the exclusive compute architecture for its upcoming "Starmind" orbital AI satellite constellation.

### 2. Key Facts & Numbers
*   **Partnership Scope**: SpaceX will use NVIDIA Rubin GPUs and Vera CPUs for the compute payload of its "AI1" satellites.
*   **Infrastructure Goal**: Starmind aims to create a distributed data center in orbit to bypass terrestrial constraints (power, land, cooling).
*   **Technical Specs**: Each AI1 satellite is designed to provide 120 kW–150 kW of AI computing power, with a physical size of 20 meters tall and a 70-meter wingspan.
*   **Financials**:

*   **Timeline**: First batch of Starmind satellites is expected to launch as early as 2027.

### 5. Macro/Geopolitical Context
The Starmind project is a response to the "energy sovereignty" and infrastructure bottlenecks on Earth. By moving AI compute to orbit, SpaceX aims to access unlimited solar energy and exploit the vacuum of space for passive cooling, effectively decoupling AI growth from terrestrial power grid limitations. This also has dual-use implications for national security, given SpaceX’s existing Starshield contracts.

### 6. Most Affected Party & Impact
*   **Gains**: **NVIDIA** (secures a massive, exclusive, high-volume customer for the Vera Rubin platform) and **SpaceX** (potentially revolutionizes AI inference costs and infrastructure scalability).
