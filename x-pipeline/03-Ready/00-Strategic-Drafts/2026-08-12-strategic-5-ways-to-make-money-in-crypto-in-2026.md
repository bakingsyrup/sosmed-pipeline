#### 2. Stablecoin Lending/Yield Farming APR (Early 2025)
- **STATUS:** VERIFIED
- **FINDINGS:** As of early 2025, stablecoin yields were highly segmented based on risk and complexity. Conservative lending on major protocols like **Aave** typically ranged from **3% to 8% APY**. More advanced strategies involving liquidity provision or yield tokenization (e.g., Pendle, Morpho) offered higher ranges, often between **5% and 12%**, with specialized or incentive-boosted strategies occasionally reaching **20–30% APY**.
- **SOURCE:** [Stablecoin Yields in 2025: Mapping Risk, Return, and Protocol Dominance](https://www.transfi.com)

#### 3. NFT Mint-to-Peak ROI (Q1 2025)
- **STATUS:** PARTIAL
- **FINDINGS:** A definitive "average mint-to-peak ROI" for all NFT launches is not tracked as a single industry metric due to the high volatility and failure rate of new collections. While major "blue chip" collections (e.g., CryptoPunks, Pudgy Penguins) experienced periodic double-digit price surges, the broader NFT market faced intense competition from memecoin trading activity, which captured the majority of retail speculative volume on Solana and Ethereum during this period.
- **SOURCE:** [Solana vs. Ethereum: Which Ecosystem Is Winning 2025 So Far?](https://www.coingecko.com)

---

#### 4. Notable Play-to-Airdrop (P2A) Campaigns
- **STATUS:** VERIFIED
- **FINDINGS:** The P2A model evolved significantly in 2024–2025, shifting from simple "play-to-earn" to points-based systems. Notable campaigns like **Pixels (Season 2)** and various tap-to-earn games (e.g., Notcoin, Hamster Kombat) dominated the space. Average token allocations varied wildly; for example, Pixels rewarded the top 7,000 players on its leaderboard, with additional distributions for random participants. Precise dollar values per player are rarely disclosed as a flat average due to the extreme variance between "bot" accounts and genuine high-engagement players.
- **SOURCE:** [The State of Play-to-Earn Projects](https://www.cryptonary.com), [Airdrop Data 2025 Report](https://www.airdropalert.com)

---

#### 5. KOL Allocation Discount Percentages
- **STATUS:** VERIFIED
- **FINDINGS:** KOL (Key Opinion Leader) rounds typically offer discounts ranging from **20% to 50%** compared to the price offered in the adjacent funding round (often the public or private round). These allocations generally represent 5–15% of a project's total raise.
- **SOURCE:** [What Is a KOL Round in Crypto? Terms & Risks](https://www.unitypad.io)
