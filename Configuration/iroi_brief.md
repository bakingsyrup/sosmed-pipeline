# Iroi Business Briefing (`iroi_brief.md`)
**Last Updated:** July 25, 2026

This document serves as the high-level strategic blueprint for **Iroi**, a multi-platform social media media and creator business.

---

## 🏛️ Business Overview & Core Vision

**Iroi** operates across social media platforms (currently prioritizing **X / Twitter**, expanding to YouTube, LinkedIn, and Threads). The business model relies on a closed-loop system combining automated production and automated intelligence.

### Closed-Loop Architecture

```
                       ┌─────────────────────────┐
                       │     Iroi Business       │
                       └────────────┬────────────┘
                                    │
           ┌────────────────────────┴────────────────────────┐
           ▼                                                 ▼
┌───────────────────────────┐                     ┌───────────────────────────┐
│          SHINKU           │                     │          BEIDOU           │
│   (Production Engine)     │                     │   (Intelligence Engine)   │
│ • Research & Verification │                     │ • Account Performance     │
│ • Copywriting & Skeletons │                     │ • Competitor Benchmarking │
│ • Asset & Video Clipping  │                     │ • Macro/Micro Diagnostics │
└─────────────┬─────────────┘                     └─────────────▲─────────────┘
              │                                                 │
              │ (Publishes Content)          (Measures Metrics) │
              └────────────────► [ PLATFORMS ] ─────────────────┘
                                   (X, YT, etc.)
```

---

## ⚙️ Core Sub-Systems

1. **Shinku (Production Engine)**:
   - Handled via `Configuration/shinku_brief.md` & `x-pipeline/`.
   - Responsible for topic research, fact verification, X post drafting (7-draft system), video skeleton scripts, and A-Z glossary databases.

2. **Beidou (Intelligence & Diagnostic Engine)**:
   - Handled via `Configuration/beidou_brief.md` & `beidou-pipeline/`.
   - Responsible for competitor tracking, normalized metric benchmarking, macro vs. micro performance diagnosis, and actionable feedback loops.

---

## 🎯 Strategic Objectives

1. **Decouple Performance from Panic**: Differentiate between industry-wide macro downturns vs. account-specific micro issues.
2. **Data-Driven Iteration**: Provide concrete performance feedback to inform Shinku's content style selection.
3. **Multi-Platform Scalability**: Build modular data contracts capable of expanding across platforms seamlessly.
