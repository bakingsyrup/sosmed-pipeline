# Iroi Business Briefing (`iroi_brief.md`)
**Last Updated:** July 26, 2026

## 🏛️ Business Overview & Core Vision

**Iroi** serves as the internal automated business engine and corporate architecture. The public-facing media brand operates under **Silvester Ad Astra** across social media platforms (currently prioritizing **X / Twitter**, expanding to YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, and LinkedIn). The business model relies on a closed-loop system combining automated production, automated intelligence, and automated competitor strategy.

## 🎯 Strategic Objectives

1. **Decouple Performance from Panic**: Differentiate between industry-wide macro downturns vs. account-specific micro issues using Beidou.
2. **Non-Destructive Additive R&D**: Expand post archetypes and test competitor hooks safely via Lulua's Post Style Bank without altering Shinku's core 7-Draft output.
3. **Monothematic Cohesive Brand Expansion**: Leverage a single core research domain (Finance, Tech & Macro) across X, TikTok, IG Reels, and YouTube Shorts under the unified Iroi media brand.
4. **Execution Roadmap & Platform Strategy**: Tracked continuously in [iroi_strategy_roadmap.md](file:///mnt/data/Obsidian%20Docs/Image%20Prompt%20Db/Sosmed-Pipeline/Configuration/iroi_strategy_roadmap.md).
