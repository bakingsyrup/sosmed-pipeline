#### 💡 Topic Curation & Relevance Principles (The Everyday User Filter)
When translating abstract taxonomy nodes into active post topics, strictly apply the **3 Everyday User Filters**:
1. **Sell the Outcome, Not the Engine:** Focus on real-world utility, user benefits, and practical results—not backend mechanics, mathematical theory, or dry technical infrastructure (e.g. summarize a 100-page SEC filing in 10s vs building a Vector DB; spot high-inflation tokens vs studying validator slashing algorithms).
2. **Target Common Annoyances:** Directly address painful daily friction and pet peeves (e.g. removing robotic "AI slop", losing money to seed phrase mistakes, inflation destroying cash value, getting dumped on by high-FDV tokens).
3. **Promise Immediate Speed, Protection, or Savings:** Highlight tangible metrics like time saved ("10 seconds"), money protected ("stop token dump REKT"), or costs cut ("replace $200/mo software").
