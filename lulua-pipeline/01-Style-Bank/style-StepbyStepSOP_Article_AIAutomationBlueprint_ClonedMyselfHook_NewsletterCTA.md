I: Where Second Brain Systems Fail
Before we dive into building automations, I want to provide a bit of context as to why I built this system in the first place.
If you're like me, you've probably tried Notion, Obsidian, and other related AI second-brain tools.
The problem isn't that these tools don't work; they do, and I've made plenty of content outlining how I use them.
However, the problem I kept running into when using tools like Obsidian is the constant manual upkeep they need to stay useful.
The moment you stop adding notes to your vault, Obsidian becomes useless.
The moment you stop updating CRMs and databases, Notion becomes useless.
And as someone who is quite busy, I frankly don't have the time to maintain AI systems, CRMs, and databases. 
So, I bit the bullet and spent weeks building a system that evolves itself as I work, carries context permanently, and holds my actual step-by-step processes rather than just storing notes about them.
If you're running into the same bottleneck when trying to automate your life with AI, I really think you'll find this article useful, and I'm excited to share my new system with you.
II: Workflow Audit 
You can't automate what you haven't identified. So before anything else, we need to list out what you actually do.
The audit itself is simple: Let AI interview you about every recurring task in your life or business, then rank them by what's worth automating first. The highest priority tasks are the ones we'll build agents/automations around, so we want to be precise here.
Starting with a profile
The best way to get started is to create a "profile" of you so Claude has full context before we start automating things.
This Claude audit prompt will conduct an in-depth interview of all your daily tasks and workflows:
AUDIT PROMPT
"Interview me to map every recurring workflow in my business and my week. Ask me one question at a time. For each workflow, establish:
- The trigger: what starts it
- The steps and tools needed to execute it
- How often I do it
- How many hours a month it takes
Then score each one from 1-5 on repeatability, and 1-5 on how 
much human judgement it genuinely requires.
Keep going until we've covered planning, meetings, invoices, 
client work, emails, content, admin and anything else recurring. 
Push me if my answers are vague.
Output everything as a table I can export."

Clone Tracker (example)
You won't identify every task you do in one session. That's fine. We're just trying to build a base here, and you can add to it over time. 
Clone Score
Once you've listed all your daily tasks, Claude will rank them using a "Clone Score." The logic is straightforward. The more time something eats and the more repeatable it is, the higher the priority it becomes for automation in the later steps.
The Workflow Audit 
Real Example
I don't just want to talk theory here; I want to give you guys a real, practical example using my sponsorship pipeline.
My real BD/sponsor pipeline
One of the things my team and I spend a lot of time on is signing deals and sponsors for my brand.
On the surface, "landing a deal" feels like one thing, but in reality it's seven separate processes (shown above), and only some of them can be automated.  
For example, researching and finding contact info can absolutely be automated, but final negotiation and content delivery can't.
By conducting a workflow audit using the prompt above, you can identify not only your daily workflows but also the exact steps within them that can and should be automated.
That's the entire point of this step.
III: Designing Specs & Skills 
Once you've identified a few things worth automating, the next step is to break down your workflows into specs and skills that agents can actually run.
There are two ways to design and create automation skills within Claude:
Talk it through
If you can explain the workflow process out loud, this is the fastest route.  
Open Claude, use the skill creator, and walk it through whichever workflow you want to begin automating. 
I personally use Whispr Flow to transcribe rather than typing, because you explain things more naturally when you're speaking.  
Spec prompt to get you started:
SPEC PROMPT  "I'm going to walk you through how I do [process] step by step.  As I explain, ask me questions about anything vague or anything I  skip over. I'll miss things I do automatically without thinking,  so push me on the gaps.  For each step, capture: - What triggers it - What tools are involved - What a good output looks like - Whether it needs my judgement or can run without me  Then turn it into a skill I can run."  The questions are the important part. You'll leave things out -  everyone does - because the steps you've internalised don't feel  like steps. That's exactly what you need the AI probing for.
2.     Teach Claude a Skill
Your second option is to record your workflow and use the teach-claude-a-skill feature to automatically build a reusable Claude skill around it.
To get started with this feature, open Claude desktop and hit "Record a skill."
Record a skill
I've been toying around with this new feature quite a bit, and one of the things I really like is that Claude can actually hear you, so while you're doing a workflow, you can actually explain the entire process.
For example, if you're creating a workflow around how you write tweets, you can actually sit down, write a tweet, and explain your entire thought process to Claude. Claude then internalises all that information and creates a reusable skill from it. 
This legit feels like training an employee on exactly how you'd do a task.
By the end of this step, the goal is to have at least a few reusable skills that you're satisfied with.
IV: Folder & Visual Map
Ok, so you've got your automation specs/skills. Now they need a place to live and a way for you to actually oversee all your workflows.
Folder Design 
Ask Claude to build you a structured business folder, or make one yourself.

A business brain folder
Stores all my meetings, finances, etc. - you can add whatever you want your AI agents to know here.
Business-brain

specs

Structure:
- One node per step in the process, connected left to right in

- Branches where the process splits, converging where it rejoins

Colour system:
- Purple nodes for steps AI executes
- Yellow nodes for steps requiring manual human input
- Green nodes for external tools (name the tool on the node)

Style: dark background, clean sans-serif, high contrast. Readable

Output as one HTML file with all CSS and JS inline, no external

**II: Audit Alur Kerja Kopi**

Anda tidak bisa mengotomatisasi apa yang belum Anda identifikasi. Jadi langkah pertama: daftarkan semua yang benar-benar Anda kerjakan.

Auditnya sederhana: biarkan AI mewawancarai Anda tentang setiap tugas berulang di kedai kopi Anda, lalu peringkatkan berdasarkan prioritas untuk diotomatisasi. Tugas dengan skor tertinggi itulah yang akan kita bangun agennya.

Mulai dengan profil kedai.
Cara terbaik adalah buat “profil” kedai kopi Anda agar AI punya konteks penuh. Gunakan prompt audit ini:

**PROMP AUDIT**  
“Wawancarai saya untuk memetakan setiap alur kerja berulang di bisnis kopi saya dan mingguan saya. Ajukan satu pertanyaan satu per satu. Untuk setiap alur, tetapkan:
- Pemicu: apa yang memulainya
- Langkah dan alat yang dibutuhkan
- Seberapa sering dilakukan
- Berapa jam per bulan yang dihabiskan  
Lalu beri skor 1–5 untuk pengulangan, dan 1–5 untuk seberapa banyak penilaian manusia yang benar-benar dibutuhkan.  
Lanjutkan sampai kita mencakup perencanaan menu, pemesanan green bean, jadwal roasting, kontrol mutu seduh, perawatan mesin, inventori, pelatihan staf, dan apa pun yang berulang. Dorong saya jika jawaban saya kurang jelas.  
Keluarkan semuanya dalam bentuk tabel yang bisa saya ekspor.”

Anda tidak akan mengidentifikasi semua tugas dalam satu sesi, dan itu wajar. Kita hanya membangun fondasi yang bisa ditambah seiring waktu.

**Skor Klon**  
Setelah daftar tugas harian terkumpul, AI akan memberikan “Skor Klon.” Logikanya sederhana: semakin banyak waktu yang dimakan dan semakin bisa diulang, semakin tinggi prioritas otomatisasinya.

**Contoh Nyata Audit Alur Kerja**  
Saya tidak ingin cuma teori – saya kasih contoh nyata dari jalur pengadaan biji kopi saya.

Salah satu hal yang paling menyita waktu tim saya adalah mencari dan menjalin kerja sama dengan petani dan importir green bean.  
Di permukaan, “mendapatkan green bean” terasa seperti satu aktivitas, tapi nyatanya terdiri dari tujuh proses terpisah, dan hanya sebagian yang bisa diotomatisasi.

Misalnya, riset asal biji dan mencari kontak bisa sepenuhnya diotomatisasi, sementara uji cupping akhir dan negosiasi harga tidak bisa.

Dengan melakukan audit seperti di atas, Anda bisa mengidentifikasi langkah-langkah persis dalam setiap alur kerja yang bisa dan harus diotomatisasi. Itulah inti langkah ini.

---

**III: Merancang Spesifikasi & Skill Otomatis**

Setelah mengidentifikasi beberapa tugas bernilai tinggi, langkah selanjutnya adalah memecah alur kerja menjadi spesifikasi dan skill yang bisa dijalankan agen.

Ada dua cara merancang skill otomatisasi:

1. **Jelaskan sambil berbicara**  
Kalau Anda bisa menjelaskan alurnya secara lisan, ini rute tercepat.  
Buka Claude, gunakan skill creator, dan pandu ia melalui alur kerja yang ingin Anda otomatisasi.  
Saya pribadi memakai transkripsi suara (Whispr Flow) karena kita lebih natural saat berbicara.

**PROMP SPESIFIKASI**  
“Saya akan memandumu melalui bagaimana saya melakukan [proses] selangkah demi selangkah. Saat saya menjelaskan, tanyakan bagian yang kurang jelas atau yang saya lewati. Saya pasti melewatkan hal yang sudah otomatis saya lakukan tanpa berpikir, jadi dorong saya untuk mengisi celah itu.  
Untuk setiap langkah, tangkap:
- Pemicunya
- Alat yang digunakan
- Seperti apa output yang baik
- Apakah butuh penilaian saya atau bisa berjalan sendiri  
Lalu ubah menjadi skill yang bisa saya jalankan.”

Pertanyaan-pertanyaan itu penting. Langkah yang sudah terinternalisasi sering tidak terasa seperti langkah; di situlah AI harus memancing.

2. **Ajari Claude Sebuah Skill**  
Opsi kedua: rekam alur kerja Anda dan gunakan fitur “Record a skill” di Claude desktop untuk secara otomatis membangun skill yang bisa dipakai ulang.

Fitur ini membuat Claude benar-benar mendengar Anda saat mengerjakan tugas sambil menjelaskan proses berpikir Anda. Misalnya, saat membuat alur kerja “kalibrasi grinder pagi”, Anda bisa langsung mendemonstrasikan sambil bicara. Rasanya seperti melatih karyawan baru.

Di akhir langkah ini, targetnya adalah memiliki setidaknya beberapa skill yang siap pakai dan Anda puas.

---

**IV: Folder & Peta Visual Operasional**

Sekarang spesifikasi dan skill sudah ada. Mereka butuh tempat tinggal dan cara agar Anda bisa mengawasi semua alur kerja.

**Desain Folder**  
Minta Claude membuatkan folder bisnis terstruktur, atau buat sendiri. Folder saya terdiri dari dua bagian:
- **Folder “Otak Kedai”**: menyimpan semua catatan rapat, keuangan, profil pemasok – apa pun yang perlu diketahui agen.
- **Folder “Spesifikasi”**: tempat menyimpan semua file skill dan SOP yang dibuat di langkah sebelumnya.

Di sinilah bagian yang sangat berguna: agen mungkin bisa membaca file markdown dengan baik, tapi Anda sebagai manusia tidak. Kita lebih suka visual, dan saat menjalankan banyak skill, mudah kehilangan jejak. Karena itu saya buat peta visual interaktif dari semua alur kerja.

**Cara Membangun Peta Visual**  
Buka Claude Cowork, jatuhkan file spec/skill Anda, lalu pakai **PROMP PEMBUAT PETA** berikut:

**PROMP PETA**  
“Baca file spesifikasi yang saya berikan dan ubah menjadi satu peta HTML interaktif.  
Struktur: satu node per langkah, tersambung dari kiri ke kanan sesuai urutan eksekusi, lengkap dengan cabang dan penggabungan.  
Sistem warna: node ungu untuk langkah yang dieksekusi AI, kuning untuk input manusia, hijau untuk alat eksternal (tulis nama alatnya).  
Interaktivitas: saat hover, highlight semua koneksi; klik node tampilkan detail lengkap (pemicu, output, alat, apakah perlu persetujuan). Tandai titik review dan approval dengan jelas.  
Gaya: latar gelap, sans-serif, kontras tinggi, mudah dibaca. Output satu file HTML dengan CSS dan JS inline, tanpa dependensi eksternal. Simpan di folder ini.”

Proses peta ini mungkin terlihat sepele, tapi sebenarnya sangat membantu: memaksa Anda berpikir kritis di mana AI benar-benar dipakai, menguraikan SOP setahap demi setahap, dan memungkinkan visualisasi serta penambahan layer bila perlu. Anda bisa menyimpan HTML ini secara lokal atau deploy via Vercel.

**V: Menerapkan Alur Kerja Otomatis**

Mari rekap sejauh ini:  
1. Anda melakukan audit waktu dan menemukan beberapa tugas high-leverage yang layak diotomatisasi.  
2. Anda merancang skill dan spesifikasi yang mereplikasi tugas-tugas itu.  
3. Anda membuat sistem folder dan peta visual untuk manajemen yang mudah.

Sekarang langkah terakhir: benar-benar menjalankan alur kerja dan agen Anda.  
Kabar baiknya, ini bagian termudah dan Anda punya banyak opsi:

Anda bebas mengombinasikan semua opsi di atas. Mulailah dengan beberapa alur kerja dulu, jangan langsung puluhan. Fokus pada tugas harian dengan dampak terbesar.

# Part 3: Master Universal Wireframe Blueprint (Elastic Narrative Phases)

## Phase 1: Giant Promise Hook
**Purpose**: Seize attention and establish extreme outcome possibility using a personal “clone” or “automation” claim.

- **Dynamic Content Scaling Directive**: Analyze the core transformation promise of the user’s topic. For topics with one clear hero result, deliver a single high-impact statement. For multi-faceted topics, break into bullet versions of the same promise.
- **Scaffold**: [Slot: Personal Outcome Claim & Big Promise], optionally include [Slot: Visual/Data Proof] if available. Always close with a bridge that declares “This is the most important [topic] article/system I’ve ever created.”

## Phase 2: Structural Roadmap & Pain Point Setup
**Purpose**: Frame the enemy (old system/solution) and set up the forthcoming step-by-step system as the definitive answer.

- **Elasticity via Pain Depth Assessment**: Scale the number of posts here based on how deeply the audience relates to the old failure. For simple pain, 1 post. For chronic pain with multiple failure points, expand to 2–3 posts to mirror each failure.
- **Core Components**: 
  - [Slot: Common Pitfall & Solution Hook] – name old tool/method, describe why it fails, state the new approach.
  - [Slot: Step-by-Step Menu Teaser] – present a table-of-contents-style preview of the phases.
- **Bridge**: End with a clear transition into the first actionable phase.

## Phase 3: Workflow Discovery & Prioritization (Audit Phase)
**Purpose**: Shift from theory to personal application by forcing the audience to audit their own workflows.

- **Dynamic Content Scaling**: Number of posts = number of distinct audit layers (trigger, tools, frequency, score, example).
  - Minimum: 1 post combining audit philosophy + prompt.
  - Maximum: separate post for audit philosophy + clone score explanation, one post for the exact prompt, one post for a real personal example with breakdown.
- **Key Slots**: 
  - [Slot: Audit Philosophy & Priority Logic]
  - [Slot: Ready-to-Use Workflow Discovery Prompt]
  - [Slot: Personal Example with Step Breakdown]
- **Bridge**: Conclude the phase by refocusing on the “exact steps worth automating.”

## Phase 4: Skill & Specification Design
**Purpose**: Convert loose workflows into concrete, reusable AI skills.

- **Scaling Variable**: 1–3 posts depending on number of alternative methods presented. Minimum: one method with prompt. Expand: second method, optional live-recording demonstration.
- **Slots per method**: 
  - [Slot: Transformation Promise & Bridge to How-To]
  - [Slot: Live Coaching Prompt Template] (verbal walkthrough)
  - [Slot: Alternative No-Write Method Description] (recorded skill)
- **Phase Conclusion**: Remind user of the goal (“at least a few reusable skills”).

## Phase 5: Organizational Structure & Visual Map
**Purpose**: Give the system a tangible, manageable form through folder design and an interactive map.

- **Dynamic Scaling**: If the topic involves a simple linear process, one post for folder + map. If complex with multiple branches, separate into 2–3 posts: folder architecture, map prompt, and map benefits.
- **Slots**: 
  - [Slot: Visual Map Blueprint & Folder Architecture]
  - [Slot: Interface Translation Prompt & Recap Transition] (the exact MAP PROMPT)
- **Blue Language**: End with a sense of “now everything is visible and under control.”

## Phase 6: Deployment Options & Conversion CTA
**Purpose**: Drive immediate action on deploying the system and convert the reader to a longer-term follower/subscriber.

- **Dynamic Content Scaling**: For simple deployment (one tool), one post. For multi-option deployment with tool names, one post listing options. The CTA always attaches.
- **Slots**: 
  - [Slot: Multi-Option Deployment Runway & Conversion Invitation]
  - [Slot: Authority Follow Prompt & Lead Magnet CTA] (newsletter, social handle)
- **Always end with**: a warm sign-off that reads like a personal note, reinforcing 100% free/no-spam if a lead magnet is involved.

**Cross-Phase Elasticity Rule for Drafting Agents**:  
Analyze the user’s input data depth. Each Phase’s post count expands or contracts based on the amount of genuine detail, examples, and alternatives available. The content MUST flow naturally; never force an artificial split. The blueprint is a skeleton, not a cage. A shallow input yields a tight 5‑post thread; a deeply documented case can bloom into 12–15 highly valuable teaching posts.
