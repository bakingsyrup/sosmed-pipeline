# Lulua Style Bank: Algorithmic Reveal Wireframe

### Part 1: Metadata & Ratio Trigger
*   **Target Metrics:** High Bookmarks (Save-ability) and High Click-Through Rate (CTR).
*   **Ratio Strategy:** The content relies on a "High-Authority/High-Result" imbalance. By pairing a specific tool (Claude) with a specific, high-value outcome (+$168k), it triggers a spike in **Bookmarks** (users wanting to replicate the process) and **Views** (curiosity gap).

### Part 2: Psychological Lever
*   **Primary Emotional Driver:** **Utility + FOMO.**
*   **Mechanism:** It positions the reader as "missing out" on a technical advantage. By claiming to have tested "twelve of the most famous strategies" and finding "only one" that works, it leverages **Authority Bias** and the **Curiosity Gap**—the reader feels compelled to learn the "secret" winner to avoid future losses.

### Part 3: Micro-Rhythm & Pacing Rules
*   **Hook Length Cap:** Keep under 15 words. The hook must state the "Who/What/Result" immediately.
*   **Line-Break Density:** High. Use single-sentence paragraphs to force the eye down the page.
*   **Sentence Caps:** Maximum 2 clauses per sentence. Keep language punchy and devoid of filler.
*   **Thread/Section Progression:** 
    1.  **The Result:** State the massive win immediately.
    2.  **The Process:** Briefly explain the "how" (the tool/method).
    3.  **The Filter:** Introduce the "Only One" constraint to create exclusivity.
    4.  **The Payoff:** Promise the reveal.
