### Part 1: Metadata & Ratio Trigger
*   **Target Metric:** Replies (Primary), Bookmarks (Secondary).
*   **Viral Mechanism:** Low-friction engagement. By asking the user to simply "Reply" or click a link, the barrier to entry is near zero. The high volume of replies signals social proof to the algorithm, boosting visibility.

### Part 2: Psychological Lever
*   **Primary Emotional Driver:** Utility & FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out).
*   **Mechanism:** The content promises "real-time trade alerts" (Utility) and leverages the "many followers have already joined" angle to create a bandwagon effect (FOMO/Social Proof).

### Part 3: Micro-Rhythm & Pacing Rules
*   **Hook Length Caps:** Keep hooks under 10 words. Direct, conversational, and slightly mysterious.
*   **Line-Break Density:** High. Use single-sentence paragraphs to force the reader's eye down the screen.
*   **Sentence Caps:** Maximum 15 words per sentence. Keep language simple and transactional.
*   **Thread/Section Progression:** 
    1.  Create a "Low-Stakes" environment (casual conversation).
    2.  Introduce the "Value Proposition" (Trade alerts).
    3.  Provide the "Immediate Action" (WhatsApp link/Keyword).
    4.  Repeat the offer (Redundancy ensures the CTA is not missed).
