# Master Wireframe: Format 11 (FounderRetrospective / Strategic Lessons)

> **Psychological Lever**: Executive Vulnerability & Long-Term Wisdom.  
> **Core Intent**: Shares personal strategic reflections from past market cycles to establish deep brand authenticity and authority.

---

## 📐 1. Elastic Narrative Rhythm

### 🔵 X (Twitter) Thread Rhythm (Dynamic 3 to N Posts)
* **Post 1 (Hook)**: Long-term reflective milestone ("Lessons learned after navigating [X] market cycles...").
* **Post 2..N (Strategic Lessons)**: 1 core lesson per post (The mistake made ➔ The strategic shift ➔ The permanent rule).
* **Final Post (Outro & Follow CTA)**: Reflection summary + [Owned Channel Handle] follow invite.

### 🟣 Instagram Carousel Spec (Dynamic 3 to 15 Cards)
* **Card 1 (Cover)**: Headline ≤ 12w, Subhead ≤ 18w (Total ≤ 30w). Style A minimal story layout.
* **Cards 2..N (Lesson Cards)**: Lesson Header ≤ 6w, Retrospective text ≤ 25w/block (Total ≤ 40w/card).
* **Card N (Outro)**: Final Thought ≤ 15w, Follow CTA ≤ 15w.
