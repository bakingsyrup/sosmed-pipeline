### Part 1: Metadata & Ratio Trigger
*   **Target Metric Trigger:** Bookmarks (High Utility/Save Value).
*   **Why:** The content positions itself as an exclusive "insider" guide to an official document, creating a "Save for later" imperative. The promise of correcting a common mistake (99% usage error) forces the user to store the post to avoid feeling behind or incompetent.

### Part 2: Psychological Lever
*   **Primary Emotional Driver:** FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) + High Utility Authority.
*   **Mechanism:** By claiming 99% of users are doing it wrong, the author creates an immediate intellectual threat. The reader saves the post to align themselves with the "1%" who know the correct methodology.

### Part 3: Micro-Rhythm & Pacing Rules
*   **Hook Line Length:** Under 10 words (High-impact opening).
*   **Line-Break Density:** Aggressive; 1 sentence per line to maximize mobile vertical real estate.
*   **Whitespace Pacing:** Double-spacing between the "Problem" (99% doing it wrong) and the "Solution" (The guide overview).
*   **Section Progression:** Chronological urgency (Time-bound) $\rightarrow$ Negative reinforcement (The error) $\rightarrow$ Value proposition (The models) $\rightarrow$ Direct CTA.
