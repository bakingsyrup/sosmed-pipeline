**Penyelaras Kulit**

### Menemukan Rutinitas Skincare yang Layak Diuji
Langkah pertama dalam sistem ini adalah mencari rutinitas perawatan yang benar-benar punya dasar. Kamu tidak perlu meracik dari nol, malah saya sarankan jangan. Mulailah dari yang sudah ada, lalu sempurnakan berdasarkan hasil simulasinya.

Kamu punya tiga opsi solid:
1. **Perpustakaan Komunitas Skincare** – Forum dan platform seperti FemaleDaily, Reddit SkincareAddiction, atau grup Facebook menyimpan ribuan rutinitas dari pengguna sungguhan yang bisa kamu pelajari, tiru, dan modifikasi. Lihat langsung testimoni dan foto sebelum/sesudahnya.
2. **Galeri Rutinitas Terbuka** – Banyak kreator dan dermatolog merilis template rutinitas pagi/malam gratis. Saya cukup mencari "rutinitas kulit sensitif pagi malam PDF" dan langsung mendapatkan 250+ template siap pakai.
3. **Bangun sendiri bersama Claude** – Kalau kamu sudah punya ide atau produk tertentu, curahkan idenya ke Claude, ubah menjadi seperangkat aturan, lalu aturan itu diubah menjadi format CatatanRutin yang bisa diimpor ke aplikasi catatan harianmu.

TL;DR: Curahkan ide rutinitas ke Claude, ubah jadi aturan (cuci – serum – pelembap – sunscreen pagi, dsb.), ubah ke format .md, impor ke CatatanRutin untuk simulasi. Tujuan fase ini bukan langsung menemukan yang sempurna, tapi mengumpulkan beberapa kandidat yang bisa kita poles. Rekomendasi saya: simpan 3-5 template dari komunitas dan galeri terbuka.

### Mengubah Strategi Kulit Menjadi Aturan Rutinitas Harian
Sekarang kamu punya kandidat; kamu perlu sesuatu yang bisa disimulasikan. Ini bukan kode, tapi template rutinitas terstruktur yang bisa dibaca mesin. Kamu tidak perlu belajar membuatnya, biarkan Claude yang menulis. Buka Claude dan tempel prompt ini:

PROMPT: "Ubah strategi skincare ini menjadi template rutinitas harian terstruktur versi CatatanRutin. Sertakan langkah pagi dan malam, tipe produk spesifik yang digunakan, frekuensi eksfoliasi, jeda antar produk, dan indikator kulit yang harus dicatat setiap hari. Buat template siap pakai agar bisa langsung saya salin ke aplikasi jurnal kulit saya."

Saya pakai kulit kombinasi sensitif sebagai dasar, tapi silakan sesuaikan prompt dengan kondisi kulit aslimu—semakin akurat, semakin presisi hasil simulasinya. Dalam hitungan detik, kamu sudah punya template lengkap.

### Menjalankan Simulasi & Mencatat Hasilnya
Di sinilah kamu bisa melihat bagaimana rutinitas itu sebenarnya bekerja. Salin seluruh template dari Claude, lalu ikuti langkah ini:
1. Buka aplikasi CatatanRutin (atau notion/jurnal fisik yang kamu punya)
2. Buat halaman baru "Simulasi Rutin 1"
3. Tempel template dari Claude
4. Mulai jalani rutinitas sesuai aturan, catat kondisi kulit pagi dan malam

Setiap hari, aplikasi akan mengingatkanmu mengisi ceklis: tingkat minyak, kemerahan, jerawat baru, tekstur, hidrasi. Dalam 2-4 minggu, kamu akan melihat pola performanya. Hal yang perlu dicatat:
- **Jumlah Jerawat Baru**: indikator utama kecocokan
- **Tingkat Iritasi/Kemerahan**: tanda produk tidak cocok
- **Konsistensi Tekstur Kulit**: perubahan halus
- **Waktu Muncul Masalah**: apakah setelah produk tertentu?

Catatan: kadang template tidak cocok di awal. Normal. Rekomendasi saya: screenshot bagian yang terasa aneh, berikan ke Claude, minta penyesuaian. Saran saya, ulangi proses ini minimal 3-5 kali dengan template berbeda, simpan yang paling cocok. Saya sendiri membangun Penyelaras Kulit dan menguji 432 template, hasilnya worth it.

### Membiarkan Claude Menganalisis & Menyempurnakan
Ini langkah yang hampir tidak ada yang lakukan, padahal di sinilah nilai sesungguhnya. CatatanRutin memberimu data mentah, tapi tidak memberi tahu *kenapa* kulitmu bereaksi. # Part 3: Master Universal Wireframe Blueprint (Elastic Narrative Phases)

**Phase 1: Authority & Outcome Hook**
- **Role**: Trigger immediate curiosity and establish credibility through a quantified personal result (earnings, months of clear skin, saved hours, etc.).
- **Core Elements**:
  1. One-liner praising the tool combo (Claude + [Industry Platform]).
  2. Pain acknowledgment ("If you're struggling to...").
  3. Personal success statement with precise metrics.
  4. "In this [article/thread], I'm revealing the entire process so you can copy it exactly."
- **DYNAMIC CONTENT SCALING DIRECTIVES**:
  - **Minimum (1 post)**: Combine all four elements into a single dense hook.
  - **Expanded (up to 3 posts)**: Dedicate one post to pain point & desire, one to the credibility proof (with a visual of the result), one to the teaser of the system and what's to come.
  - **Adaptation rule**: If input data contains multiple proof pillars (e.g., revenue + testimonial + screenshot), expand; if only one metric, compress.

**Phase 2: Value Stack & Incentive Preview**
- **Role**: Explicitly list the learning outcomes and front-load the lead magnet teaser to incentivize reading till the end.
- **Core Elements**:
  1. "What you'll learn:" followed by 3-5 bullet points (outcome-oriented).
  2. "If you stick around until the end, I'll send you [lead asset name]—[one-line value prop]."
  3. Visual placeholder or brief description of the lead asset.
  4. Transition line: "With that out of the way, let's jump right in."
- **DYNAMIC CONTENT SCALING DIRECTIVES**:
  - **Minimum (1 post)**: Combine bullets, teaser, and visual mention into one post.
  - **Expanded (up to 2 posts)**: One post for learning outcomes, one for incentive deep-dive including a 'sneak peek' image or testimonial.
  - **Adaptation rule**: If lead asset has multiple high-value components (templates, calculators, community access), expand into multi-angle teaser.

**Phase 3: Step-by-Step SOP (Adaptive Phases)**
This phase is broken into one sub-phase per step. The number of steps is determined by the source (here 4 steps). Each step can scale independently.

**Phase 3.1 – Step 1: Discovery / Sourcing**
- **Role**: Show where to find high-quality starting points, remove invention pressure.
- **Core Elements**:
  1. Step title & justification (why this step is critical).
  2. Statement that you don't need to create from scratch.
  3. 3 concrete source options with short explainer (Public Library, Repository, Build with AI).
  4. TL;DR summary of the fastest path.
  5. Bridge to next step.
- **SCALING**: **Minimum (1 post)** – combine all options into a concise list post with TL;DR. **Expanded (3 posts)** – one post per option with detailed how-to and screenshots; a fourth TL;DR post. **Adaptation rule**: If source data provides direct links or examples, build separate posts per option.

**Phase 3.2 – Step 2: Translation / Codification**
- **Role**: Convert the idea into a processable format (script, template, checklist).
- **Core Elements**:
  1. "You don't need to learn [technical language], just use Claude."
  2. Exact copy-paste prompt with variable slots (commission, portfolio size, skin type, etc.).
  3. Note on customization for accuracy.
  4. Outcome statement: "Within seconds, you'll have a complete [output]."
- **SCALING**: **Minimum (1 post)** – prompt + quick customization note. **Expanded (2 posts)** – one post explaining the concept, one post with the full prompt and parameter explanation. **Adaptation rule**: If the prompt is complex with multiple configurable parameters, separate the template from the explanation.

**Phase 3.3 – Step 3: Testing / Simulation**
- **Role**: Walk through the testing environment and key metrics.
- **Core Elements**:
  1. Step-by-step visual guide (open tool, paste, run).
  2. Example output screenshot (real or representative).
  3. "Things to look for" checklist with 4-5 metrics definitions.
  4. Error handling note (if it doesn't compile, copy error to AI).
  5. Recommendation to repeat with multiple candidates.
- **SCALING**: **Minimum (1 post)** – compress steps and metrics checklist. **Expanded (up to 4 posts)** – separate posts for the tool walkthrough, error handling, metrics drill-down, and a "why sample size matters" post. **Adaptation rule**: Scale based on the richness of visual aids and metric complexity.

**Phase 3.4 – Step 4: AI Analysis & Refinement**
- **Role**: Unlock the 'why' behind performance, create a feedback loop.
- **Core Elements**:
  1. "This is the step almost nobody does, where the real value is."
  2. Instruction to export data (CSV or log) and hand it to AI.
  3. List of exact strategic questions to ask (4–5 prompts).
  4. Explanation that AI reveals root causes, improving operator skill.
  5. Loop back: "From here, you repeat the process… making it better."
- **SCALING**: **Minimum (1 post)** – combine export instruction and question prompts. **Expanded (3 posts)** – one post for the philosophy of AI analysis, one for the step-by-step export + upload, one for the question bank and expected insights. **Adaptation rule**: If the source includes sample AI insights, create a dedicated post showcasing the exchange.

**Phase 4: Lead Magnet Conversion (Research Desk / Tool Giveaway)**
- **Role**: Transform educational value into a lead exchange that automates the entire SOP.
- **Core Elements**:
  1. Pain reminder: "99% [struggle] without knowing if it works."
  2. Positioning the lead asset as the solution that automates the entire system.
  3. "I open-sourced my entire personal [asset]—here's what it does."

  5. Reciprocity statement: "This guarantees I can keep bringing high-value free content."
- **SCALING**: **Minimum (1 post)** – all elements condensed into a single conversion post with link. **Expanded (2–3 posts)** – one post detailing the asset features with images, one post for social proof (number of users or strategies loaded), and one final CTA post. **Adaptation rule**: If the lead magnet has a complex dashboard or multiple modules, create a mini-showcase before the CTA.
