## Finding Strategies Worth Testing

You do not need to invent something from scratch. Ideally, you start with something that already exists, then improve it over time.

### Three sources for strategies:
- TradingView's Public Library — thousands of free, open-source strategies
- GitHub repos — 250+ Pine Script strategies ready to go
- Build your own with Claude — brain dump your idea, Claude converts it to rules

---

## Turning Strategies Into Pine Script

Paste this prompt into Claude:

```
You are a Pine Script developer. Convert this trading strategy into Pine Script v5 that can be imported into TradingView:

[Paste strategy rules here]

Requirements:
- Portfolio size: $100,000
- Entry conditions: [describe]
- Exit conditions: [describe]
- Risk per trade: 1% of portfolio
```

> Pro tip: if the code doesn't compile on the first attempt, copy the error message, paste it back into Claude, and ask for a fix.

## The Numbers That Matter

| Metric | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Net Profit | Positive across all timeframes |
| Win Rate | Above 45% |
| Max Drawdown | Below 20% |
| Profit Factor | Above 1.5 |
| Number of Trades | At least 100 |

## Letting Claude Analyse & Refine

This is the step almost nobody does — and it's where the real value is.

Instead of manually reviewing hundreds of trades, export your complete trade history as a CSV from TradingView, upload it into Claude, and ask:

1. "Why did this strategy underperform?"
2. "Which market conditions caused the biggest losses?"
3. "How can I reduce the drawdown without overfitting?"
4. "How would you improve this strategy?"

Claude explains **why** your strategy performed the way it did. That's the part that makes you a better trader.
